Reading does not need a perfect corner, a long list, or an hour of silence. A small, repeatable moment is enough to help a child connect books with comfort.

## Begin with ten minutes

Choose a time that already belongs to your family—after dinner, before bed, or on the bus ride home. Let the book wait there, like a friendly invitation.

## Let children have a say

Offer two books and let your child choose. Choice gives a young reader ownership, even before reading feels easy.

## Keep it light

Re-read favourite pages. Talk about a picture. Stop while everyone is still enjoying it. A reading life grows through pleasure, not pressure.
