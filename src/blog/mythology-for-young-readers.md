Indian mythology carries remarkable characters, questions, and images. For young readers, the best beginning is often a story rather than a lesson.

## Start with wonder

Ask what your reader notices: a brave choice, a surprising creature, a difficult question. There is no need to explain every detail at once.

## Choose an accessible retelling

Look for clear language, thoughtful context, and artwork that welcomes a child in. A good retelling can become a bridge to deeper conversations later.

## Read together

Family reading lets children ask questions freely. It also makes room for the many ways these stories are understood and loved.
